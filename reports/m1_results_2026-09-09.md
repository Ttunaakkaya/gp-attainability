# M1 — recorded holonomic QP validation

9 September 2026. Independent implementation; development/control checks, **not**
ECC2025 reproduction, M6 held-out comparisons, or physical-robot safety evidence.

## Outcome and reproducibility

All **54 policy runs in 18 comparisons completed**: six longer pilot runs followed
by 48 development-matrix runs. The audit recomputed every executed linear segment
from raw positions, checked its linked control command, and verified serialized
records and source snapshots. No rejected command was executed.

- [Validation index, individual metrics and audited paths](../outputs/m1-20260909/validation/batch-20260909T193408322909Z-97a485/validation.json)
- [Frozen validation program](../outputs/m1-20260909/validation/batch-20260909T193408322909Z-97a485/validation_program.py)
- [Current repeatable validation command](../scripts/validate_m1.py)

From the project root, rerun without overwriting existing bundles:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts/validate_m1.py
```

The program creates a unique batch directory and unique comparison directories,
writes an incremental index, and retains failed missions as partial records. It
does not render videos or start training. Its default output is
`outputs/m1-20260909/validation`; `--output` can select another destination.

The pilot uses four robots, combined loss/disturbance, seed 7, and 300 simulated
seconds, once per controller. The subsequent matrix is:

`2/4 robots × nominal/combined × seeds 7/19 × filter/QP × three policies`,
with 90 simulated seconds per policy. The policies remain sweep, nominal greedy,
and adaptive greedy; **B3 timed DP and the proposed budget mechanism are not present**.

All conditions use the existing 60 × 40 m field, exact GP, 0.5 s motion updates,
5 s sensing period, 2 m/s speed limit and 2 m minimum pair separation. Combined
conditions use 30% measurement loss and 0.35 m/s standard-deviation velocity
perturbations. Perturbations enter the requested command **before** either control
layer; these runs do not model unknown post-QP disturbances.

QP is not uniformly better at mapping. For example, adaptive greedy with two
robots in combined conditions at seed 7 has **higher** RMSE under QP by 0.011664,

does not establish method superiority, and posterior variance is not a guarantee
of field error. No confidence interval or significance claim is drawn from these
two development seeds.

  starting positions and applied velocities. Failed events have no applied command.

The QP acceptance tolerance is 1e-7; the reference-filter audit uses 1e-9. Reported
tiny speed/domain excursions are numerical deviations, not exactly zero. The QP
uses an inscribed 16-sided speed polygon, not an exact circular speed constraint.
CBF and conservative separating-plane checks apply to the declared disturbance-free
piecewise-linear execution after command processing, not physical USV trajectories.

## Preserved failure and unchanged reference behavior

The [initial four-robot pilot](../outputs/m1-20260909/initial-pilot/20260909T192223157488Z-58b6fedc-914075/comparison.json)
is retained with its original source snapshot and three failed partial missions.
Its solver used relative tolerance `eps_rel=1e-8`: large CBF rows increased OSQP's
relative termination scale, permitting a 1.23392781e-7 speed-facet residual although
the solver reported `solved`. Independent acceptance correctly rejected it.

The correction was **stricter solving**, not relaxed motion acceptance:
`eps_rel=0`, `eps_abs=1e-8`, unchanged acceptance 1e-7. The failing geometry is now
a QP regression test. The initial failure bundle separately passed all 26 manifest
hash checks and failed-prefix trajectory/control audits. Failed prefixes were not
padded with invented stationary motion or compared as full-horizon mapping scores.

The six matching filter runs (combined, seed 7, two/four robots) were also compared
against the preserved [v0 scaling-validation evidence](../outputs/package-validation-20260908/validation_evidence.json).
All **9,774 original motion fields and 342 sample records matched exactly**.
The 114 map frames and non-timing quality summaries matched except planned-variance
roundoff of at most **5.55111513e-17**. The reference controller's executed behavior
is preserved; new telemetry and statuses do not replace old results.
