Fixed target or switching margin?

When the current plan predicts missing the mission target, should an already evaluated, feasible route that reaches it take priority over the usual preference to keep the route? This demo isolates that choice.

These numbers are invented for explanation. This is not research evidence or a recovery guarantee. Every forecast here describes a route followed by holding at its end, under nominal motion. It does not predict the ordinary adaptive planner's future decisions.
